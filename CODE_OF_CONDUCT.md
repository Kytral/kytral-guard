# Code of conduct

We want Kytral Guard to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Expected**: be kind and patient; assume good intent; give and take constructive criticism; focus on what is best for the
users of the project; credit others' work.

**Not acceptable**: harassment, insults, personal or political attacks; sexualized language or imagery; publishing
someone's private information without permission; deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, pull requests or
discussions.

**Enforcement**: maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do not
follow this code. Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting (Security tab, or a
direct message to a maintainer); reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
